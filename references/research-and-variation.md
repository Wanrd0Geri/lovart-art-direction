# Research and Variation

Use research to extract design logic, not to decorate a prompt with titles or artist names.
Treat convergence toward familiar forms as a production risk to inspect, not as a universal model law.

## Research evidence

For each real case, record:

- Project or work name.
- Author, studio, or project owner.
- Publishing platform.
- Direct source link.
- Confirmed tool or production method, when available.
- Visible and executable dimensions worth learning from.
- Specific subject, composition, brand, or protected expression that must not be copied.

Mark unavailable facts as `未确认`.
Never infer a tool, engine, or author from visual appearance alone.
Prefer primary portfolios, studio pages, official showcases, talks, documentation, and project breakdowns.
Use secondary coverage only when it adds attribution or production context unavailable from a primary source.

Convert each useful case into medium, shape, construction, material response, palette, light, value, density, and function.
Do not use a living artist's or studio's name as the operative style instruction.

## Style cards

Give every selected direction:

- Medium and image-making logic.
- Shape language.
- Primary materials and state-appropriate surface variation.
- Color relationships.
- Light and value structure.
- Detail-density pattern.
- Functional or construction logic.
- Cultural, historical, or technological rules.
- Research basis and source links.
- One structural difference from every other selected direction.

Do not count weather, time of day, or a palette swap as a distinct style by itself.

## Subject pool

Make every subject function-specific and immediately designable.
Prefer `药圃`, `矿井`, `渡口`, `铸造坊`, `盐井`, or `驿站` over `各种场景` or `修炼地点`.
Build a larger internal pool when useful, but put only selected numbered subjects and a small reserve into the final prompt.
Do not bury Lovart under an unfiltered research dump.

## Positive originality

Activate explicit originality work only when the user asks for it, research targets differentiation, the pool is repetitive, or previous results show convergence.
Keep every user-requested motif as a fixed item.
Differentiate through function, geography, material culture, construction, social use, ritual, ecology, scale, and spatial logic.
Use concrete positive alternatives instead of a long negative-prompt blacklist.

## Variation matrix

Separate:

1. Fixed attributes: world rules, medium, cultural family, identity anchors, material system, and within-group style.
2. Planned variation: region, occupation, function, terrain, faction, technique phase, camera state, or narrative state.
3. Forbidden drift: unauthorized culture, identity, medium, core material, or reference-role changes.

Choose three or four reasonably independent axes.
Test independence by changing one axis value: if that forces another axis to change, split or redefine the axes.
Assign axis values and one unique difference to every numbered deliverable.

## Research output

For `research-by-host`, embed only verified links, compact findings, and selected executable attributes.
For `research-by-lovart`, require Lovart to separate confirmed facts, uncertain claims, design rules, and non-copyable elements before generation.
For every mode, prohibit invented works, authors, links, production methods, or claims of market consensus.
