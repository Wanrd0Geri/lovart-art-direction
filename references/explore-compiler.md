# Explore Compiler

Use only for `route=explore` when the direction is unresolved or the user requests research, comparison, or a visual system before production.

## Research ownership

- `research-by-host`: verify first through available primary or authoritative sources; embed compact findings and direct links.
- `research-by-lovart`: instruct Lovart to research, cite sources, mark unavailable facts as unconfirmed, and avoid invented cases.
- Do not use `no-research` when the user requires real attribution, market cases, or verified production methods.

Never treat a blocked fetch as proof that a source does not exist.
Never infer the production tool, author, or method from appearance alone.

## Style cards

For every direction record:

- medium and image-making logic;
- shape language;
- materials and state-appropriate surface variation;
- color relationships;
- light and value structure;
- detail-density pattern;
- construction and functional logic;
- cultural, historical, or technological rules;
- verified research basis and direct links when applicable;
- one structural difference from every other direction;
- protected expression that must not be copied.

Weather, time of day, and palette swaps do not constitute independent directions.
Living creator, studio, genre, and work names stay in the research layer; convert them into visible attributes before image generation.

## Release behavior

Choose `research-then-review` when direction approval is required.
Choose `research-then-execute` when the user explicitly requests an uninterrupted run.
After selection, compile the approved direction through the build contract without forwarding research prose, names, sources, or uncertainty notes to the image model.
